  1. Tap a boat, on the map or in the strip, to follow it.
  2. Press ▶ to replay the race from the start. Drag the slider to go back in time, and use the speed button to go faster.
  3. The round buttons: trails, wind, highlights, fit all, share and import.
  4. With a boat selected, ⇄ compares it with another boat.

Unofficial fan tracker made by Raphael Ribard, built with AI assistance. Not affiliated with the Route du Rhum organisers, the teams or the skippers.

Positions come from an unofficial relay of the public tracking feed plus community history, and may be late or wrong. Wind is model data from Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0) on a coarse grid. Coastlines are simplified drawings.

Import positions

This page cannot reach the race website on its own. Paste a position report (JSON or CSV) or choose a file. Every import adds to the trajectories, and speed and course are worked out from consecutive positions when missing.

Positions are read from an unofficial relay, not from the race organisers. They update about once an hour. This page is not affiliated with the Route du Rhum.
